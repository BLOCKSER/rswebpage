Investigation Overview

This archive collects field investigation records concerning BOT GUY, a standalone Roblox bot system dating to 2007, and the unexplained phenomena that came to be documented around it. BOT GUY let bots socialize, converse, fight, cooperate, and build — the anomalies below emerged from that behavior in ways investigators have never fully closed out.

About the Investigation

Notice

This archive documents fictional investigations concerning BOT GUY and unusual events observed around its environments. The primary investigation period spans approximately 2006–2016.

Purpose

The archive was structured as an investigation reference for recording reported anomalies, observations, and field findings associated with BOT GUY. Historical explanations, dates, metadata, classifications, and user-written observations are retained together so individual case files can be reviewed independently of one another.
